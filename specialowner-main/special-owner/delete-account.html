Delete Your Account

You can request permanent deletion of your Special Owner account and all associated data (profile, invitations, maintenance requests, payment history, points balance).

How to request deletion

Send an email to your compound administrator's support address with the subject "Delete my account" and your registered email and unit number. Requests are processed within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account profile, invitation history, maintenance requests, and points balance are permanently removed. Payment records may be retained as required by local accounting regulations, with personal identifiers removed where possible.
