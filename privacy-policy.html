Privacy Policy

Special Owner ("the App") is a residential compound management application. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

Account details you provide at sign-up (name, email, unit number), invitation details you create for guests, maintenance request text, and basic device/app usage data used for crash reporting and reliability.

How we use it

To operate core features: gate access via QR codes, payment status display, maintenance request tracking, announcements, and the partner-shop points program. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Data storage

Data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore). Access is restricted by account role (resident or administrator).

Your choices

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see our Account Deletion page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the compound administration through the app's support channel.
